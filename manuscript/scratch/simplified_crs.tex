If $B = (R,L)$ is a weighted bipartite graph with adjacency matrix $M$ and having the following properties:
\begin{itemize}
	\item{$\forall u \in S, S \subseteq R,$ deg($v$) = $|L|$}
	\item{$\forall u \in S, \forall v \in L, w((u,v)) = 1$}
\end{itemize}

then per($M$) = per($M'$) $\times \binom{|L| - |R|\setminus|S|}{|S|}$, where $M'$ is the adjacency matrix of the subgraph of $B$ induced on all vertices adjacent to edges of weight $\geq$ 1.
